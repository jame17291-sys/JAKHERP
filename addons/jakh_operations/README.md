# JAKH connected contracting operations

Approved project requisitions reserve released WBS budgets and create linked native purchase orders. Technical staff manage permitted quantities and evidence without financial-field access. Controlled service entries synchronize accepted subcontract scope, cap payable matching and preserve reversal evidence. Approved subcontract variations amend confirmed orders and commitments without rewriting prior accepted rates.

Finance agreements govern supplier advances, recovery and retention. Funded advances require posted bills and actual reconciled payments; SES-derived certificates create gross cost and separate control-account deductions. Retention releases require available posted liabilities. Customer commercial documents similarly govern funded advances, cumulative certificates, retention release and final accounts. Native full credits correct posted evidence; posted financial sources cannot be silently rewritten.

Accepted labour and plant costs create balanced native journals exactly once, linked to project-cost facts. Resource allocations validate company/site/WBS scope and overlapping capacity. Versioned P6/Jisr/plant intake retains provenance, validates payloads, logs failures and supports idempotent retry; normal business approval remains mandatory. Integration adapters and live vendor acceptance remain deployment-specific.

CVR, forecasts, cumulative P6 earned value, CPI/SPI and scheduled CSV/XLSX snapshots use permission-scoped source calculations. Financial reports read posted journals. Company direct cash movements reconcile cash balances and flag incomplete classifications; statutory indirect cash flow and approved consolidated reporting remain separate work. Final closure requires settled commercial documents, completed scope and no pending project/field approvals. Production/localization gates require real customer evidence and do not infer acceptance from synthetic tests.

The supported transaction basis is a single legal-entity currency, primary product UOM and explicit configured tax/account policy. Foreign-currency contracting, external report delivery, banking synchronization, identity federation and production e-invoicing need their respective approved integrations and acceptance evidence. See the package README and requirements register for the current engineering scope and unsigned customer gates.
